# Kyndryl (kyndryl)
Kyndryl is a Fortune 500 IT infrastructure services company spun off from IBM in 2021. The company designs, builds, manages, and modernizes mission-critical technology systems across cloud, mainframe, networking, digital workplace, security, applications, data, and AI. Kyndryl operates the Kyndryl Bridge integration and observability platform, runs the Kyndryl Vital design studios, and partners with hyperscalers including AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and SAP.
